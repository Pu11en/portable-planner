# Prove the decisive planning flow

Type: implementation and validation
Status: beta-5 objective repairs pass; awaiting fresh human acceptance
Blocked by:

## Question

Can Portable Planner stay brief, stop low-value grilling, act immediately on explicit authority, and push finished work into the right test without taking protected human decisions?

## Confirmed behavior

- Record explicit scoped delegation and synthesize its reversible decisions; repeated agreement alone is not authority.
- Research facts, ask only uncovered human tradeoffs, and switch experiential uncertainty to one bounded ordinary/contrasting/failure trial.
- Keep ordinary replies to a few short lines and perform the next safe action in the same turn.
- Treat `yes` to a direct approval question as explicit authorization and immediately begin the normal harness build when safe.
- Preserve irreversible commitments, personal tradeoffs, conflicts, implementation authorization, and final approval as human gates.
- After agent checks pass, proactively present the smallest genuine user test.
- After three consecutive acceptances of recommended reversible choices, explicitly offer scoped delegation when at least two such choices remain; never infer it from the pattern.
- After a digression, answer or reconcile it first and then restore the current one-letter choice path at the bottom of the reply when a human decision remains.
- Keep research-derived changes to the product destination, source role, or output provisional until the person confirms them.
- Refresh all generated state before a live test, reuse known context, and ask at most one truly blocking test-input question.
- Treat a presentation failure separately from the plan source and immediately use a supported readable fallback.

The normative decision is [P-001](../../planning/decisions/P-001-define-decisive-planning.md), supported by [research and scenario evidence](../../planning/evidence/P-001-evidence.md).
