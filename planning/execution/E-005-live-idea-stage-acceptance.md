# E-005 — Run live idea-stage acceptance

- Outcome: Drew uses the installed feature on one genuine thin software idea and confirms whether the optional scan provides a worthwhile, faster start to a credible MVP plan.
- Depends on: E-004

## Context

- [Confirmed behavior](../decisions/P-001-define-idea-evidence-flow.md)
- [Acceptance checklist](../../docs/ACCEPTANCE.md)
- [Contributor live-proof boundary](../../AGENTS.md)

## In scope

- Start from a fresh ordinary Codex task and a genuine thin or directionless software idea supplied naturally by Drew, without fixture coaching.
- Record the opening permission experience, any minimum-context question, research trace and bounds, provisional direction and alternatives, Drew's confirmation or redirect, resulting planning consequence, transition into ordinary planning, confusion, friction, weak claims, and Drew's direct judgment.
- Verify one result link, evidence tier, and reuse or constraint claim manually before acceptance.
- Record every live failure before changing architecture; fix and rerun only the affected behavior until the live checklist honestly passes or remains blocked.
- Reconcile `docs/ACCEPTANCE.md`, project-map state, and public-preview status with the resulting evidence.

## Out of scope

- Building the sample idea, executing or installing found repositories, private-repository research, replacing the existing first-real-use and complex-plan acceptance obligations, or claiming the feature is proven from synthetic evidence alone.

## Constraints

- The experience must remain optional, bounded, compact, and useful even when the answer is that no repository should be reused.
- No surfaced link or claim may be fabricated; no public repository is treated as safe or reusable without explicit evidence and license qualification.
- Portable Planner still stops at the approved plan and normal-harness build handoff.

## Proof

- A linked raw live transcript and planning artifacts show the complete behavior from opening through ordinary planning.
- The scan stays within the approved default or records the exact justified exception; each surfaced repository changes the MVP route; at least one claim and source round-trip manually.
- Drew explicitly judges whether the scan was worth its time and reading load, dynamically fit his actual input rather than replaying a template, improved understanding of what was possible, and produced a stronger credible planning direction.
- Every applicable acceptance check is updated with linked evidence and no unresolved failure is mislabeled as pass.

## If blocked or disproven

- Preserve the last passing core planner behavior, record the concrete failure, and return only the affected trigger, research, result, or safety rule to planning. Do not add infrastructure merely because a live run failed.

## Human review

- Required: Drew supplies the real idea, reviews the research result and resulting MVP route, opens at least one source, and gives the explicit acceptance judgment.

- Plan complete.
