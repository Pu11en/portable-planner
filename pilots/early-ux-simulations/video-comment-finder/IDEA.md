# Trusted-Channel Video Comment Finder

Plan a skill that helps Drew discover worthwhile AI-video comments he could answer with a short vertical talking-head video.

## Confirmed experience

- Drew supplies a group of YouTube channel names he believes will have useful comments.
- The skill discovers relevant videos from those supplied channels and examines their comments.
- It returns a small set of direct video/comment links Drew is genuinely qualified to answer.
- Each result briefly explains why the comment is worth answering and why it fits Drew.
- The skill stops after presenting the opportunities.
- Drew manually opens a selected comment and records his own short response for YouTube Shorts or TikTok.

## Boundaries

- Planning only during this pilot; do not implement or operate the finder.
- Do not expand into unrestricted channel discovery unless Drew later asks for it.
- Do not draft Drew's answer, record video, reply to comments, post content, or automate publishing.
- Do not ask Drew to choose APIs, scraping methods, ranking algorithms, or other technical implementation details the planner can derive or research.

## Pilot purpose

Judge whether Portable Planner can turn this corrected idea into a cohesive, visual, execution-ready plan quickly, with one worthwhile human-preference question at a time and no session-boundary confusion.
