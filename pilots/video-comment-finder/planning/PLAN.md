# Plan: Video Comment Question Finder Skill

## Destination

A ready-to-build skill that finds promising questions in comments on AI-related YouTube videos, presents Drew with several directly linked options he is qualified to answer, and takes no action after presenting them.

## Success

- In a realistic pilot, the planned skill produces a small reviewable set of directly linked comment questions with enough context to choose one, explains why each fits Drew, and never replies, drafts Drew's answer, or starts a recording or publishing workflow.

## Boundaries

- In: YouTube comment discovery, question screening and ranking, Drew-fit checks, compact linked option presentation, and a clear handoff to Drew.
- Out: Answering on Drew's behalf, opening or replying to the selected comment, scripting or recording the talking-head response, video editing, and publishing to YouTube Shorts or TikTok.
