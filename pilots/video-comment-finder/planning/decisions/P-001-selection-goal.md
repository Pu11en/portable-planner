# P-001 — Selection goal

- Status: current
- Depends on: none

## Decision

Set the primary meaning of "worthwhile" so later search and ranking rules optimize for Drew's intended result instead of an arbitrary popularity signal.

## Viable options

- Best short-form answer: favor questions Drew can answer clearly and usefully in a brief vertical video; strongest viewer value, with less trend-chasing.
- Reach potential: favor popular or fast-moving questions and threads; may surface broader opportunities but can overvalue engagement.
- Authority fit: favor questions that most strongly showcase Drew's distinctive knowledge or experience; strengthens positioning but may narrow the pool.
- A custom weighted mix of these goals.

## Recommendation

Prioritize the best short-form answer, then use authority fit and reach potential as secondary signals. This preserves usefulness as the core promise while still allowing strong positioning and timely opportunities to break ties.
