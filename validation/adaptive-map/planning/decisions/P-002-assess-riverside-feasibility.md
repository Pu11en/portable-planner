# P-002 — Resolve Riverside Park Feasibility

- Status: complete
- Depends on: P-001

## Decision

Use the venue manager's completed site assessment and direct decision to settle the venue route, remove obsolete work, and activate Drew's saved fallback preference if the park is denied.

## Viable options

- Not applicable — use the direct venue decision rather than asking Drew to choose equipment, research procedure, or the already-settled fallback again.

## Recommendation

Accept the denial, move the unchanged event to the offered Riverside Community Center, and remove the outdoor branch. The indoor venue preserves the fixed date and capacity target while eliminating outdoor power, screen, sound, and weather work.

## Confirmed decision

Riverside Park is denied because no screen placement satisfies both emergency access and the sound boundary. Use Riverside Community Center on the same date and time at no charge. Plan for its posted capacity of 100, built-in projector and sound system, accessible entrances and restrooms, marked center aisle, and two unobstructed exits.

- Execution starts by documenting the community-center reservation and constraints, then handles licensing and budget, indoor operations, communication, readiness, and outcome proof.
- Built-in projection and sound are used; equipment rental is not part of the route unless a later direct test disproves their adequacy and returns the plan for revision.
