# E-004 — Release Attendee Communication

- Outcome: Neighbors receive accurate launch and reminder messages, with responses tracked against the 60-attendee target.
- Owner: Agent prepares and schedules materials; the human event lead authorizes and sends them.
- Depends on: E-002, E-003

## Context

- [Licensed title and budget](E-002-license-movie-and-budget.md)
- [Operations package](E-003-build-operations-package.md)

## In scope

- Prepare a launch message and reminder cadence naming the licensed movie, Riverside Community Center, September 12, 2026, 7:00–10:00 PM, free admission, capacity limit, accessibility, and the instruction to bring a blanket or chair.
- Use every available no-cost, organizer-authorized channel from this bounded list: the organizer's neighborhood email or contact list, the neighborhood's online group, and the community center's calendar or notice board. Record any channel that is unavailable or denied.
- Track deliveries, responses, accessibility requests, questions, and projected attendance.
- Correct or retract any message if venue, license, or safety facts change.

## Out of scope

- Food sales, fundraising, admission charges, outdoor-weather messaging, unrelated promotion, and event operation.

## Constraints

- Do not announce before E-001 through E-003 are proven.
- Do not promise attendance beyond the room's posted capacity of 100.
- Messages must match the written venue, license, accessibility, and bring-your-own-seating facts.

## Proof

- Approved launch and reminder copies, send records, response tracker, and a current projected-attendance count exist; every published fact traces to the confirmed venue, license, or operations package.

## If blocked or disproven

- Use any remaining available channel from the bounded list when response is weak; return to planning if projected attendance cannot reasonably reach 60 without changing scope, budget, or event terms.

## Human review

- The human event lead approves the message before the first send and any material correction.
