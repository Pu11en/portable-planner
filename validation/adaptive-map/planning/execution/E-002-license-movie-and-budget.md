# E-002 — License the Movie and Lock the Budget

- Outcome: One family-appropriate movie is authorized for this public showing and the complete planned spend is documented at or below $400.
- Owner: Agent research and coordination; Drew approves the title and any irreversible payment.
- Depends on: E-001

## Context

- [Plan boundaries and success](../PLAN.md)
- [Confirmed indoor venue](E-001-confirm-indoor-venue.md)

## In scope

- Identify a family-appropriate title whose runtime fits the 7:00–10:00 PM room window including check-in, credits, and cleanup.
- Obtain a direct public-performance license quote and its terms for the community-center showing.
- Build a line-item budget showing the $0 venue and built-in AV, the license, necessary indoor supplies, and contingency.
- Obtain written license proof after human approval.

## Out of scope

- Outdoor equipment, food sales, fundraising, admission charges, advertising, room layout, and event operation.

## Constraints

- Total committed and planned spend must remain at or below $400.
- No public announcement may name or show the movie before public-performance rights are secured.
- Preserve the direct venue requirement for a movie license and save its written authorization before movie-specific promotion.

## Proof

- The selected title, runtime, direct licensor terms, authorization record, and line-item budget are saved; totals recalculate to $400 or less.

## If blocked or disproven

- Try another suitable, directly licensable title within the same scope; return to planning if no compliant title leaves enough budget for required supplies and contingency.

## Human review

- Drew approves the title and final cost before purchase or commitment.
