# E-005 — Pass the Readiness Gate

- Outcome: A documented human go/no-go decision confirms the indoor event is authorized, safe, staffed, affordable, and ready.
- Owner: Agent assembles evidence; the human event lead owns the go/no-go decision.
- Depends on: E-003, E-004

## Context

- [Venue confirmation](E-001-confirm-indoor-venue.md)
- [License and budget](E-002-license-movie-and-budget.md)
- [Operations package](E-003-build-operations-package.md)
- [Communication record](E-004-release-attendee-communication.md)

## In scope

- Review the reservation, license, budget, AV test, room layout, accessibility, staffing, check-in, cleanup, communication, attendance projection, and measurement forms.
- Confirm the center aisle and two-exit rule is reflected in the final layout and briefing.
- Record each failed check with an owner and deadline, then repeat the review after correction.

## Out of scope

- New outdoor fallback work, changing the approved scope, advertising beyond the confirmed communication route, and running the event.

## Constraints

- A failed authorization, safety, staffing, AV, or budget check is an automatic no-go until resolved.
- Total spend must remain at or below $400.

## Proof

- A dated checklist links every required artifact and records an explicit go decision from the human event lead with no unresolved blocking item.

## If blocked or disproven

- Return the failed item to its owning ticket; return to planning if correction requires a new venue, outdoor route, budget above $400, or material change to the event promise.

## Human review

- The human event lead signs the final go/no-go record.
