# E-006 — Operate, Close, and Measure the Event

- Outcome: The indoor movie night is safely completed and its attendance, retention, compliance, spend, and cleanup results are recorded.
- Owner: Human event team performs physical operations; an agent may coordinate the runbook and consolidate evidence.
- Depends on: E-005

## Context

- [Approved operations package](E-003-build-operations-package.md)
- [Passed readiness gate](E-005-pass-readiness-gate.md)
- [Plan success conditions](../PLAN.md)

## In scope

- Follow the approved setup, room layout, check-in, capacity, safety, playback, accessibility, incident, and cleanup procedures within the venue access window.
- Count total attendance and the number remaining when credits begin.
- Record any venue-rule or safety incident, final line-item spend, room handback, and cleanup confirmation.
- Calculate whether attendance reached 60, retention reached 80%, violations remained at zero, and spend remained at or below $400.

## Out of scope

- Changing venues, adding outdoor equipment or weather work, selling food, fundraising, charging admission, or improvising around a failed safety or authorization check.

## Constraints

- Keep the marked center aisle and both exits unobstructed for the full event.
- Never exceed the room's posted capacity of 100.
- Stop playback or admission when required to protect safety, license terms, or venue rules.

## Proof

- The completed check-in count, credits-retention count and percentage, incident/rule record, final spend total, cleanup handback, and success-condition summary are saved together.

## If blocked or disproven

- Follow the operations package's stop procedure for any live safety, authorization, capacity, or equipment failure; record the facts without concealing a missed success condition.

## Human review

- The human event lead signs the closeout and the venue contact confirms acceptable room handback.
