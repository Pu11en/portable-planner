# E-001 — Confirm the Indoor Venue

- Outcome: A written, execution-ready reservation record for Riverside Community Center on September 12, 2026, from 7:00–10:00 PM.
- Owner: Agent coordination with the venue manager; human review before accepting new terms.
- Depends on: P-002

## Context

- [Selected venue decision](../decisions/P-002-assess-riverside-feasibility.md)
- [Direct venue evidence](../evidence/P-002-evidence.md)

## In scope

- Obtain written confirmation of the date, time, no-charge room use, posted capacity of 100, built-in projector and sound, accessible entrances and restrooms, marked center aisle, and two required unobstructed exits.
- Record access, setup, teardown, cleanup, key or staff contact, and any additional written room rules.

## Out of scope

- Riverside Park, outdoor permits, generators, outdoor screens, weather planning, movie licensing, and attendee communication.

## Constraints

- Preserve the September 12, 2026, 7:00–10:00 PM event, free admission, expected 80-person audience, and $400 total cap.
- Do not accept a conflicting venue term or pay a venue fee without returning to planning.

## Proof

- A dated reservation or direct manager confirmation records every listed venue fact, access window, responsible contact, cleanup duty, and any added rule.

## If blocked or disproven

- Return to planning if the room is unavailable, costs money, has capacity below 80, lacks usable accessible facilities, or imposes a rule that conflicts with the event or success conditions.

## Human review

- Drew approves any newly introduced venue term before it becomes binding.
