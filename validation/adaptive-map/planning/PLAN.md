# Plan: Riverside Community Movie Night

**Status:** awaiting approval

## Destination

A complete, executable plan for a free indoor family movie night for about 80 neighbors at Riverside Community Center on September 12, 2026, from 7:00–10:00 PM.

## Success

- At least 60 people attend, at least 80% of attendees stay through the credits, there are no safety or venue-rule violations, and total spending stays at or below $400.
- Another agent can coordinate the approved route through session-sized execution tickets, with the named human reviews and physical event roles.

## Boundaries

- In: community-center confirmation, movie selection and public-performance licensing, budget control, indoor room and equipment planning, attendee communication, check-in, show operation, cleanup, and outcome measurement.
- Out: Riverside Park, generators, outdoor screens or anchoring, sound-spill mitigation, rain monitoring, outdoor weather fallback, food sales, fundraising, and admission charges.
- Planning does not run or advertise the event. Attendees bring their own blankets or chairs.
