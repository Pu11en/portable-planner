**Date:** 2026-08-05

While preparing the real simple pilot, an opening containing orientation, one literal example comment, and one complete `A/B/C` choice set measured 86 words. The implementation task initially treated that as a failure and proposed a strict 80-word ceiling.

Drew corrected the standard:

> I don't care about their words I think 86 words is fine anything under 100 is fine But yeah like a standard near 60 is fine but like don't be strict please

Confirmed product interpretation:

- roughly 60 words is a flexible design target;
- an ordinary reply under 100 words is acceptable;
- useful orientation, concrete examples, and meaningful tradeoffs must not be cut merely to satisfy a counter; and
- requested visuals, evidence, and explicit artifacts may be longer.

The proposed F-012 failure was retracted. The 86-word clean pilot opening remains valid.
