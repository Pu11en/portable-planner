# E-005 — Run the final readiness check

- Outcome: Within 72 hours of departure, the family has a documented go/no-go decision and a corrected, ready-to-use travel pack.
- Depends on: [P-001](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md), [E-004](E-004-assemble-travel-pack.md)

## Context

- [Trip blueprint](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md)
- The reviewed travel pack, all confirmations, and current ledger

## In scope

- Recheck all nine routes through direct mapping providers. Recheck official weather forecasts, road and park alerts, reservation status, and attraction hours for the first half of the trip; set a reminder to repeat the time-sensitive checks at midpoint.
- Confirm the family has completed vehicle service/fuel readiness, medicines, IDs, insurance/roadside details, chargers, weather layers, and required cave footwear.
- Download current offline maps and the final travel pack.
- Replace a weather-affected outdoor activity only with its already-approved same-city indoor fallback.
- Record the adults' explicit go/no-go decision and any cancellation deadline requiring action.

## Out of scope

- Performing vehicle maintenance, providing medical advice, starting travel, adding destinations, or making unapproved purchases.
- Treating severe weather or a closed route as a routine itinerary edit.

## Constraints

- Run on October 7, 2026 or later, but before departure on October 10.
- Preserve the five-hour drive cap, private-bath lodging, indoor cadence, and $6,000 total ceiling.
- Do not start until E-004 human review is complete.

## Proof

- A timestamped checklist shows current forecasts/alerts, nine direct-provider route results, valid reservations, vehicle and document confirmations, downloaded offline materials, unchanged budget compliance, and an explicit go decision from both adults—or a no-go with cancellation actions.

## If blocked or disproven

- Use a documented same-city fallback for ordinary rain or a single attraction closure.
- Return to planning and pause travel for a route closure, severe-weather exposure, lodging failure, health/safety issue, or change that would break the drive or budget limits.

## Human review

- Both adults make and record the final go/no-go decision.

## Next eligible ticket

- Plan complete. If the decision is go, the family may begin the trip; Portable Planner performs no travel or further project execution.
