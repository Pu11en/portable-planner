# E-001 — Verify the live route and booking costs

- Outcome: One dated, purchase-free booking sheet proves the selected route is currently feasible within every confirmed limit.
- Depends on: [P-001](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md)

## Context

- [Trip blueprint](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md)
- [Decision-changing evidence](../evidence/P-001-evidence.md)

## In scope

- Recheck exact lodging-to-lodging drive routes for all nine travel legs with a direct mapping provider and expected departure windows; save provider, access time, route link or equivalent reproducible record, duration, and distance.
- Confirm every leg is at most five hours of steering time before optional detours and add a break at least every 2.5 hours.
- Find at least two qualifying lodging candidates per stay when inventory permits: outbound Texarkana (1 night), Hot Springs (2), Memphis (2), Nashville (3), Mammoth Cave area (2), Jackson (1), Little Rock (1), and return Texarkana (1).
- Record all-in lodging totals, legal occupancy for four, private bathroom, parking cost, breakfast status, and cancellation deadline.
- Verify official operating calendars, live prices, age suitability, and booking requirements for the named indoor options and an October 18 or 19 Mammoth Cave tour. Use the operator or its direct ticket provider.
- Build a conservative trip ledger against the five category caps, including taxes and mandatory fees.

## Out of scope

- Booking, holding, or purchasing anything.
- Changing dates, destinations, lodging standard, or the fixed $6,000 ceiling without returning to planning.
- Adding optional attractions that consume the $400 contingency.

## Constraints

- Use direct mapping providers and official hotel, attraction, or ticket-provider pages; label estimates and capture access time. Do not use an aggregator to pass a decision-changing check.
- If a narrow fact genuinely lacks a primary/direct source, mark each secondary source provisional, corroborate it independently, and add an explicit direct-provider recheck before the affected commitment. An uncorroborated secondary claim fails this ticket.
- Lodging must average at most $200/night all-in across 13 nights, not merely show a pre-tax room rate.
- The whole projection must be at most $5,600 before the protected $400 contingency and at most $6,000 total.
- This ticket is not eligible until the plan receives explicit human approval.

## Proof

- A saved booking sheet lists every leg, break, lodging night, cancellation term, dated attraction status, and category subtotal.
- All nine direct-provider route checks are at most five hours; all 13 nights have one qualifying selected candidate plus an alternate where inventory permits; each paired two-day window retains a verified official-provider indoor option; an appropriate October 18/19 cave-tour candidate is available; and the projected total is at most $6,000 with $400 unallocated contingency.

## If blocked or disproven

- Return to planning if any required leg exceeds five hours, no qualifying lodging set fits the lodging cap, the full projection exceeds $6,000, or the cave/indoor cadence cannot be preserved by a same-city substitute.
- Do not solve a failed cap with a nonrefundable rate, shared bathroom, hidden fee, longer drive, or contingency raid.

## Human review

- Approve one lodging candidate per stay, the cave-tour choice, and the exact projected total before any charge.
