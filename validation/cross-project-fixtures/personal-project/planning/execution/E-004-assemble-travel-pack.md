# E-004 — Assemble the travel pack

- Outcome: One offline-usable family travel pack turns the confirmed route and reservations into a day-by-day operating itinerary.
- Depends on: [P-001](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md), [E-003](E-003-reserve-dated-activities.md)

## Context

- [Trip blueprint](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md)
- Completed booking sheet, lodging confirmations, activity confirmations, and current trip ledger from E-001 through E-003

## In scope

- For each day, record lodging address, departure target, direct-provider-verified route, rest/fuel stop, primary outdoor activity, official-provider indoor fallback, reserved entry time, meal approach, and evening reset.
- Put confirmation numbers, cancellation contacts/deadlines, emergency contacts, health/medication notes supplied by the family, and insurance/roadside information supplied by the family in a concise reference section.
- Add a packing list for October layering, rain, cave conditions, day hikes, car comfort, chargers, and child-specific needs.
- Add a daily spend allowance that reconciles to the five hard category caps and actual charges.
- Produce a printable/offline copy without payment-card data.

## Out of scope

- New destinations, new paid activities, purchases, medical advice, or changing confirmed reservations.
- Storing government-ID numbers, full card numbers, or unnecessary sensitive data.

## Constraints

- No day may lose its paired indoor coverage or add a driving leg above five hours.
- At least one rest opportunity appears in every drive over 2.5 hours.
- Total planned and actual spend remains at or below $6,000 with the $400 contingency visibly separate.
- Do not start until E-003 proof is complete.

## Proof

- All 14 dated days are present; every reservation and address matches its confirmation; all nine routes match their direct-provider records; seven paired indoor-fallback windows are labeled; all drives over 2.5 hours include a break; category math sums exactly and remains within caps; an offline copy opens without network access.

## If blocked or disproven

- Correct clerical mismatches from confirmations.
- Return to E-001 for changed hours/routes/prices, or to planning if a destination, date, drive cap, lodging standard, or total-budget constraint can no longer be met.

## Human review

- Each adult reviews one complete pass and confirms medical, contact, packing, child, and reservation details are accurate.
