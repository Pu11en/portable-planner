# E-003 — Reserve dated activities

- Outcome: Capacity-limited activities are confirmed and every paired two-day indoor fallback remains usable within the $650 activity cap.
- Depends on: [P-001](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md), [E-002](E-002-secure-refundable-lodging.md)

## Context

- [Trip blueprint](../decisions/P-001-trip-blueprint.md)
- [Decision-changing evidence](../evidence/P-001-evidence.md)
- Actual lodging confirmations and updated ledger from E-002

## In scope

- Recheck NPS and Recreation.gov immediately before checkout, then reserve a family-suitable Mammoth Cave tour for October 18 or 19, preferring Mammoth Passage, Frozen Niagara, or a comparable verified option.
- Reserve timed museum entries only where advance entry is required or materially reduces sellout risk.
- Keep free/pay-at-door indoor alternatives documented for each paired two-day window.
- Have the human complete or explicitly authorize each purchase and record confirmation, refund/change terms, date, time, party size, and amount.
- Update the activity and total-trip ledgers after each charge.

## Out of scope

- Strenuous, crawling, age-ineligible, or four-hour cave tours.
- Premium add-ons, impulse activities, dining reservations with deposits, or spending contingency on routine admission.
- Any lodging change.

## Constraints

- Total activity spending, including taxes and fees, is at most $650.
- The human controls every purchase.
- Book the cave tour first; optional paid museums yield to the cave tour and the total budget.
- Use the attraction operator or its direct ticket provider for every decision-changing schedule, suitability, price, and refund claim. If a narrow claim requires provisional secondary evidence, independently corroborate it and obtain the direct-provider answer before purchase; otherwise stop.
- Do not start until every E-002 lodging reservation is confirmed.

## Proof

- A confirmation exists for the cave tour and every other purchased timed activity; all party ages are eligible; all dates align with lodging; the indoor-fallback matrix covers all seven paired two-day windows; the activity subtotal is at most $650 and the full trip remains at most $6,000.

## If blocked or disproven

- Use another family-suitable cave tour on October 18 or 19 if available.
- Return to planning if no family-suitable cave tour exists on either date or maintaining the indoor cadence would break the total budget; do not silently replace the cave destination or exceed a cap.

## Human review

- Verify names, ages, dates, times, refund terms, and activity total against the confirmation emails.

## Next eligible ticket

- [E-004 — Assemble the travel pack](E-004-assemble-travel-pack.md), only after reservation proof and human review are complete.
