# E-004 — Build Facilitator Delivery Kit

- Outcome: Equip Drew and two helpers to deliver the fixed agenda, beginner support, reviews, fallbacks, and incident response consistently.
- Depends on: P-001, E-003

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- Completed participant kit and confirmed venue record.

## In scope

- Create a minute-by-minute run sheet, room/setup diagram, staff role cards, helper clinic rotation, review allocation, time cues, materials list, teardown list, and communications tree.
- Script the opening data-safety briefing, workflow demonstration, review criteria, suspected-exposure response, connectivity fallback, and schedule recovery.
- Run a timed staff tabletop and revise unclear ownership or handoffs.

## Out of scope

- Changing the outcome or agenda blocks, recruiting attendees, purchasing, or replacing professional emergency/venue procedures.

## Constraints

- Maintain three staff, the 9:00–4:30 boundary, two attendee breaks and lunch, one passed review per attendee, and confidential-data prohibition.

## Proof

- Complete delivery packet and tabletop record with all three staff roles, each agenda handoff, all 25 reviews, fallbacks, and incident actions assigned and timed.

## If blocked or disproven

- Do not advance while a responsibility, fallback, or review load is unowned. Revise the kit or return to planning if three staff cannot deliver it. E-005 stays ineligible.

## Human review

- Drew and both helpers acknowledge their role cards and the tabletop corrections.
