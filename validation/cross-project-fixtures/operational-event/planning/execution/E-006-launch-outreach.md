# E-006 — Launch Outreach

- Outcome: Launch a reviewed local recruitment campaign with source-tagged registration tracking and a scheduled capacity check.
- Depends on: P-001, E-005

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- Human-approved live registration page and outreach claims.

## In scope

- Prepare a concise event listing, direct invitation, partner-forwardable note, scholarship note, and two follow-ups for local small-business-owner channels.
- Build a permission-checked recipient/channel list from authorized contacts and public submission paths.
- Add source tags, launch only after Drew approves copy and recipients, and record sends/publications and scheduled checks.

## Out of scope

- Purchased contact lists, unsolicited bulk messaging, unsupported outcome claims, price changes, new paid media, or contacting anyone before approval.

## Constraints

- Messages must accurately state the event, beginner level, laptop/access prerequisites, safe-data restriction, $149 price, five scholarships, refund/transfer policy, and limited capacity.

## Proof

- Approved copy set, permission/source record, publication/send log, functioning tagged links, and a dashboard showing registrations by source against the 25-seat target.

## If blocked or disproven

- Stop any unapproved or misdirected message, correct the source/copy, and document recovery. E-007 stays ineligible until a valid launch and tracking record exist.

## Human review

- Drew approves all public copy and the exact recipient/channel list before any message is sent or listing is published.
