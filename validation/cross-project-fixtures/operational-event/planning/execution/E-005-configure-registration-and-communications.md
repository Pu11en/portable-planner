# E-005 — Configure Registration and Communications

- Outcome: Publish a tested registration path that manages 20 paid seats, five scholarships, waitlist movement, readiness intake, and scheduled attendee communications.
- Depends on: P-001, E-004

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- Confirmed date/venue, participant prerequisites, and public safety language.

## In scope

- Configure the event page, $149 standard inventory, five full-scholarship awards, total 25-person cap, waitlist, refund/transfer rules, receipts, and contact channel.
- Collect only necessary contact, accessibility, laptop/access readiness, and workflow-interest data; prohibit confidential business/customer data in forms.
- Create confirmation, prerequisite, T-14, T-7, T-2, and post-event message templates.
- Test standard, scholarship, waitlist, refund, transfer, capacity, and mobile/desktop paths before publication.

## Out of scope

- Unreviewed publication, payment-model changes, broad customer-data collection, outreach sending, curriculum changes, or overselling capacity.

## Constraints

- The page must state date/time, bring-a-laptop requirement, approved-service/account prerequisite, safe-data rule, refund/transfer policy, accessibility contact, and what attendees will leave with.

## Proof

- Test transactions/screenshots or equivalent logs for each path, a field-by-field data-minimization review, scheduled message records, correct inventory limits, and the human-approved live registration URL.

## If blocked or disproven

- Do not publish or collect money until every critical path passes. Repair the configuration or return to planning; E-006 remains ineligible.

## Human review

- Drew approves public claims, price, scholarship language, form fields, payment/refund behavior, and the final publish action.

## Next eligible ticket

- E-006 — Launch Outreach.
