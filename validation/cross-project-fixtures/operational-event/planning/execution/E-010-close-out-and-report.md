# E-010 — Close Out and Report

- Outcome: Complete attendee follow-up and deliver a reconciled report of workshop outcomes, safety, feedback, revenue, and expenses.
- Depends on: P-001, E-009

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- Workshop attendance/review evidence, incident log, feedback, registration/payment records, and expense ledger.

## In scope

- Within two business days, send each attendee their workflow materials, safe-next-use reminder, feedback acknowledgement, and support boundary after Drew reviews the message.
- Reconcile attendance with passed reviews; resolve only non-sensitive record discrepancies.
- Reconcile gross ticket revenue, refunds/fees, every expense, and pre-revenue commitments against the $4,000 ceiling.
- Produce an anonymized outcome report covering confirmation/attendance, completion, incidents, feedback themes, budget, and specific improvements for any future planning cycle.

## Out of scope

- Publishing private attendee data, claiming unmeasured business impact, silently rewriting P-001, starting a second event, or treating proposed improvements as approved decisions.

## Constraints

- Retain only necessary records and remove confidential content if any was accidentally captured, following applicable incident and retention requirements.
- Report failures and budget variance honestly; ticket revenue never excuses a pre-revenue commitment above $4,000.

## Proof

- Dated follow-up send log, reconciled attendee/review count, resolved or actively owned incident record, final revenue/expense ledger, anonymized outcome report, and improvement log.

## If blocked or disproven

- Keep the project open while material financial, attendance, or incident discrepancies remain. Escalate plan-changing lessons into a new planning cycle rather than altering the approved plan retroactively.

## Human review

- Drew approves attendee follow-up, final financial reconciliation, incident disposition, and the outcome report.

## Next eligible ticket

- Plan complete.
