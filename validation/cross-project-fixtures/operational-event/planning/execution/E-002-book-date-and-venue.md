# E-002 — Book Date and Venue

- Outcome: Confirm one qualifying Saturday and venue in writing without breaching the controlled budget.
- Depends on: P-001, E-001

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- E-001 direct-source dossier and approved selection.

## In scope

- Reconfirm availability, inclusions, accessibility, food rules, total fees, payment schedule, cancellation terms, and connectivity-test access.
- Obtain Drew's approval of the exact contract and expense before commitment.
- Complete the authorized booking/hold, schedule an on-site internet/power/display test, and update the expense ledger.

## Out of scope

- Venue changes outside E-001 criteria, curriculum, registration publication, or any commitment that makes the $4,000 total impossible.

## Constraints

- Use the approved venue/date only; commitments must preserve every protected budget line or use documented reallocation inside $4,000.

## Proof

- Signed confirmation or paid hold, receipt, written inclusions and cancellation terms, test appointment, calendar record, and ledger showing total authorized cash commitments no greater than $4,000.

## If blocked or disproven

- Do not substitute silently or spend. Return to E-001 if availability or terms changed; return to planning if no compliant option remains. E-003 stays ineligible.

## Human review

- Drew explicitly approves the final terms and every external payment before it is made.
