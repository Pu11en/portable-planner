# E-009 — Operate the Workshop

- Outcome: Run the Saturday workshop from 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. so every attendee present leaves with one passed, repeatable, data-safe workflow card.
- Depends on: P-001, E-008

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- Signed go decision, final roster, participant kit, facilitator kit, venue record, and incident procedure.

## In scope

- Set up and check in attendees, deliver the fixed agenda, enforce the opening safety agreement, and route beginner support through the assigned clinics.
- Ensure each attendee runs one workflow twice with safe sample data, receives a recorded facilitator review, corrects any failed check, and exports/retains the final workflow card.
- Record attendance, anonymized completion, incidents, feedback, and same-day expenses; perform teardown and venue handback.

## Out of scope

- Confidential-data exercises, unapproved services, unreviewed completion claims, extra attendees, schedule extension beyond 4:30, custom consulting, or changing the plan to hide a failure.

## Constraints

- Stop any suspected confidential-data exposure immediately and follow the incident procedure. Do not record confidential content in evidence.
- A person counts as complete only after the required two runs and passed facilitator review.

## Proof

- Dated attendance record, one passed checklist per attendee present, matching anonymized completion total, incident log including zero when applicable, feedback export, expense updates, teardown check, and venue handback confirmation.

## If blocked or disproven

- Protect people and data first, follow venue/emergency procedures, and record the non-sensitive facts. Never waive review or safety checks; E-010 uses the actual result and keeps unresolved incidents open.

## Human review

- Drew signs the event record; each helper signs their assigned review set and incident/teardown responsibilities.

## Next eligible ticket

- E-010 — Close Out and Report, after workshop records are reconciled.
