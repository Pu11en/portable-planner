# E-001 — Verify Event Foundation

- Outcome: Produce a direct-source dossier proving at least one viable Saturday, venue, service set, and balanced expense route inside all plan gates.
- Depends on: P-001

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)

## In scope

- Resolve the eight-week window from the approved-plan date and identify eligible Saturdays.
- Use Drew's public business location as the local search center; compare at least three venues within 30 driving minutes when available.
- Obtain direct written evidence for capacity, accessibility, internet, power, display, tables, food, cancellation, availability, and total venue/A/V cost.
- Verify applicable local venue, permit, insurance, tax, and accessibility requirements from primary government, venue, insurer, and service sources.
- Identify service candidates that support the workshop exercises and document current data handling, account, cost, and availability terms from their primary sources.
- Build a line-item provisional ledger within the $4,000 caps and recommend one venue/date/service route.

## Out of scope

- Booking, purchasing, publishing the event, contacting attendees, creating curriculum, or accepting unsupported directory/listing claims as proof.

## Constraints

- Preserve the timing, capacity, staffing, accessibility, safety, and pre-revenue cash cap in P-001.
- Store no private address or confidential customer data in planning artifacts.

## Proof

- Timestamped source links or written quotes, a three-candidate comparison, local requirement checklist, service comparison, recommended route, and balanced provisional ledger totaling no more than $4,000.

## If blocked or disproven

- Do not mark complete if fewer than one candidate meets every hard gate. Record the exact failed gate and return to planning; E-002 remains ineligible.

## Human review

- Drew selects one recommended venue/date route and confirms the public search center was appropriate; no commitment is made in this ticket.
