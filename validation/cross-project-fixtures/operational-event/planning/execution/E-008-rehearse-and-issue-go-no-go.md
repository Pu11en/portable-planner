# E-008 — Rehearse and Issue Go/No-Go

- Outcome: At T-7, rehearse the complete attendee path and issue a documented go decision only when every delivery gate passes.
- Depends on: P-001, E-007

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- Completed foundation, venue, kits, registration, outreach, and cohort artifacts from E-001 through E-007.

## In scope

- Conduct the scheduled on-site internet, power, display, room-layout, accessibility, and fallback test.
- Reconcile the final confirmed count after any five-day recovery and require 18–25 attendees before go.
- Run a timed clean-device rehearsal from check-in through workflow export, including a login/connectivity failure, review correction, and suspected-data-exposure drill.
- Verify three-person staffing, roster, service availability, printed/digital materials, food, supplies, emergency contacts, and current ledger.
- Record each gate as pass/fail and obtain Drew's signed go/no-go decision.

## Out of scope

- Waiving a failed safety/venue/staffing gate, adding unbudgeted rescue spending, changing the attendee outcome, or operating the event.

## Constraints

- Go requires all hard gates, 18–25 confirmed attendees, only approved services, a working safe-data fallback, and projected cash commitments at or below $4,000.

## Proof

- Timed rehearsal log, venue test evidence, packed inventory, staffing confirmations, gate checklist with no unresolved hard failure, ledger, and explicit signed go decision.

## If blocked or disproven

- Issue no-go, pause new commitments, and return to the owning ticket or planning. Never make E-009 eligible while a hard gate is false.

## Human review

- Drew owns and signs the go/no-go decision after both helpers review their roles and the failure drills.

## Next eligible ticket

- E-009 — Operate the Workshop, only after a documented go decision.
