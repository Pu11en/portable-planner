# E-007 — Prepare the Confirmed Cohort

- Outcome: Reconcile and prepare the T-14 cohort, launching the fixed five-day recovery outreach in the same session if fewer than 18 are confirmed.
- Depends on: P-001, E-006

## Context

- [P-001 operating model](../decisions/P-001-workshop-operating-model.md)
- Registration roster, intake responses, participant kit, and venue/access constraints.

## In scope

- At T-14, reconcile paid, scholarship, transfer, cancellation, and waitlist records without exceeding 25 confirmed seats.
- Send the approved prerequisite/access check and data-safety reminder; record completion without collecting secrets or confidential business data.
- Convert each attendee's non-confidential workflow interest into a starter lane and allocate helper clinics and accommodations.
- If fewer than 18 are confirmed, launch the preapproved five-day targeted waitlist/partner follow-up and schedule E-008's final count.

## Out of scope

- Inventing attendee answers, collecting passwords or business/customer data, promising individualized professional advice, exceeding capacity, changing price, or unlocking rehearsal below the gate.

## Constraints

- Roster access is limited to event operations. Workflow examples and intake must use generic descriptions and safe sample data only.

## Proof

- Data-minimized roster and dated count, completed access/readiness statuses, accommodation routing, workflow-lane assignments, waitlist actions, unresolved-contact list, and—if below 18—a send/publication log for the fixed five-day recovery plus the scheduled T-7 recheck.

## If blocked or disproven

- Keep this ticket incomplete if records, safe access, or an accommodation gap cannot be routed. A below-18 count may complete only when the fixed recovery is actually launched; E-008 performs the final count and must issue no-go if it remains below 18.

## Human review

- Drew privately reviews the capacity count, accommodations, sensitive exceptions, and any decision to return to planning.

## Next eligible ticket

- E-008 — Rehearse and Issue Go/No-Go, only after every applicable proof item passes.
