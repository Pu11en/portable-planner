# Plan: Chat to Action

**Status:** awaiting approval

## Destination

Five export-ready vertical talking-head videos, each under 60 seconds, that show curious small-business owners how an AI agent differs from browser chat and where human review belongs.

## Success

- Exactly five 9:16 final exports play correctly and each has a measured runtime below 60 seconds.
- Across the set, the spoken and on-screen content explicitly covers response versus workflow, planning and tool use, a suitable task, the weekly lead-follow-up example, and review before consequential action.
- In the final review, a person can use only the videos to name the lead-follow-up task as agent-suitable, explain why it is more than a chat reply, and identify approval before sending as the review checkpoint.

## Boundaries

- In: five scripts, simple fabricated-data screen examples, presenter and screen capture, plain captions/overlays, editing in one session, five final exports, and a final comprehension review.
- Out: publishing or platform setup, paid assets, live business-system integrations, use of real customer data, autonomous sending, additional videos, and post-publication analytics.
