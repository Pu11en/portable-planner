- Outcome: A verified final folder containing exactly five accepted, correctly ordered short-form videos.

## Context

- [Plan destination, success, and boundaries](../PLAN.md)

## In scope

- Correct bounded caption, overlay, audio-level, crop, ordering, and trim defects identified in E-004 review.
- Verify file playback, 9:16 dimensions, runtime below 60 seconds, caption accuracy, and numbering for all five exports.
- Check the full set against the five confirmed episode jobs and every success condition in `PLAN.md`.
- Run the final comprehension review: using only the videos, the reviewer names the lead-follow-up task, explains reply versus workflow, and points to approval before sending.
- Record acceptance results and place only the five accepted masters in the final-delivery folder.

## Out of scope

- New creative direction, new factual claims, substantial rerecording, additional deliverables, publishing, platform metadata, live integrations, or post-publication measurement.

## Constraints

- Do not accept a video with a factual contradiction, exposed real data, missing review checkpoint, or runtime of 60 seconds or more.
- Bounded remediation may not change the approved series route or hide a failure to meet it.
- Final files remain unposted until a separate publishing decision or workflow authorizes it.

## Proof

- An acceptance record lists each final file, dimensions, exact runtime, playback pass, caption pass, data-safety pass, episode-job pass, and any remediated defect.
- The final folder contains exactly five numbered masters and no rejected draft.
- The comprehension review records all three required answers without information outside the videos.

## If blocked or disproven

- Return to E-004 for a bounded edit fix, E-003 for a required pickup, or P-001 if a factual or comprehension failure requires changing the approved route; do not waive a failed acceptance check.

## Human review

- The human owner explicitly accepts the five-file set after reading the acceptance record and watching every final export.
