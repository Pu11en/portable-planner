# E-004 — Edit the Five Videos

- Outcome: Five captioned 9:16 review exports completed in one editing session.
- Depends on: P-001, E-003

## Context

- [Series route and guardrails](../decisions/P-001-lock-series-route.md)
- E-001's approved scripts
- E-002's overlay and shot manifest
- E-003's selected presenter and screen takes

## In scope

- Build one simple vertical visual treatment, then apply it consistently to all five videos.
- Cut pauses and unusable fragments while preserving the approved spoken meaning.
- Add the planned screen inserts, concise overlays, and burned-in captions.
- Balance speech level, keep screen examples legible, and use simple cuts or restrained transitions.
- Export one numbered review file per video and record its runtime.

## Out of scope

- New footage except a separately authorized pickup, new scripts or claims, elaborate motion graphics, paid music or assets, platform posting, thumbnails, analytics, or alternate aspect ratios.

## Constraints

- Complete all five edits in one editing session using a shared treatment.
- Every export must be 9:16 and below 60 seconds.
- Captions must match the spoken words; overlays must not overstate agent capability or obscure the presenter and demo.
- No real customer data or live-system identifiers may appear.

## Proof

- Five numbered review exports open, play end to end, are 9:16, and each measures below 60 seconds.
- Captions, overlays, and screen inserts match the approved scripts and manifest.
- Speech is intelligible at a consistent level and every on-screen example is readable on a phone-size preview.

## If blocked or disproven

- Fix bounded edit defects within this ticket; request a pickup from E-003 for missing or unusable footage; return to E-001 or P-001 if meeting runtime requires removing a confirmed teaching beat.

## Human review

- The presenter watches all five review exports once and records only objective defects or approved small delivery/edit corrections for E-005; a route or claim change returns to planning.

## Next eligible ticket

- E-005 — Verify and accept the exports.
