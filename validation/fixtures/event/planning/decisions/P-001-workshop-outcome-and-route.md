# P-001 — Workshop Outcome and Route

- Status: current
- Depends on: none

## Decision

Set the concrete result each owner should achieve during the workshop, then use it to build the complete route for recruiting, preparing, delivering, and following up on the event. The attendee outcome is the highest-leverage unresolved choice because it determines the agenda, exercises, facilitator support, technology, materials, and proof of success.

## Viable options

- One working AI workflow: each owner builds and tests one workflow on a real business task. This creates the deepest hands-on result but covers fewer use cases.
- Broad practical confidence: owners try several common AI tasks and learn how to choose among them. This offers more breadth but a less tangible finished result.
- Ready-to-use business playbook: owners leave with prompts, policies, and a prioritized action plan. This creates reusable structure but allows less time for live implementation.
- A different owner-defined outcome remains viable.

## Recommendation

Optimize for one working AI workflow per owner. It best matches “hands-on” and gives the day an observable completion standard, with the tradeoff that the workshop will explore fewer AI use cases.

## Confirmed decision

Not confirmed. The user must choose or define the attendee outcome; do not infer this preference.

## Effects

- The confirmed outcome will set the agenda shape, exercise depth, facilitator-to-attendee support, participant prerequisites, materials, technology, and success measures.
- After confirmation, derive the remaining event route within this ticket; split the map only if research, prototyping, size, or dependencies prevent reliable completion in the session.
- Once the route passes the finish audit, generate dependency-ordered execution tickets covering preparation through follow-up.

## Complete when

The attendee outcome is explicit; the agenda, recruitment, registration, venue and technology, facilitation, materials, costs, contingencies, success measures, and follow-up form one coherent route; contradictions and dependencies are resolved; execution tickets cover that route; and `PLAN.md` plus `NEXT.md` agree.
