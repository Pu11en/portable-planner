# P-001 — Define the Pilot Win

- Status: current
- Depends on: none

## Decision

Choose the primary result that will make the first repair café worth running. This determines later choices about capacity, item limits, volunteer roles, event flow, and proof.

## Viable options

- A. Safe, useful pilot — Optimize for safe handling, useful outcomes even when an item cannot be fixed, and enough evidence to decide whether to repeat the event. Balanced and recommended, but it will not maximize any one headline number.
- B. Repairs completed — Optimize for the number or share of items fixed during the afternoon. This gives the clearest output measure, but can undervalue diagnosis and favor easier repairs.
- C. Community connection — Optimize for resident turnout, volunteer participation, and neighbors helping one another. This creates a welcoming event, but gives weaker proof that the repair operation itself works.

## Recommendation

A — A safe, useful pilot best fits a first event: a repair or a trustworthy diagnosis can both create value, while the afternoon also tests whether the format deserves to be repeated.

## Confirmed decision

Not confirmed.

## Evidence

- No external evidence needed for this preference decision.

## Effects

- Sets the event's success measures and influences capacity, accepted items, volunteer mix, safety controls, feedback collection, and the later execution route.

## Complete when

The primary result is explicit, the plan's destination and success proof reflect it, and the downstream planning route has been reconciled around it.
