# Plan: Saturday Digital Confidence Workshop

**Status:** planning

## Destination

An approved, run-ready plan for a free two-hour Saturday library workshop where adults with mixed devices and confidence levels learn to complete one everyday digital task safely on their own.

## Success

- The plan defines the participant promise, delivery format, attendance limits, staffing, registration, two-hour flow, safety boundaries, accessibility, contingencies, and an observable way to confirm that each participant can repeat their task independently.

## Boundaries

- In: Planning the first workshop around the library's existing room and Wi-Fi.
- Out: Running the workshop, purchasing new technology, or performing participants' tasks for them.

## Map

- ▶ [P-001 — Define the workshop and complete the plan](decisions/P-001-define-workshop.md) — depends on: none

## Confirmed decisions

- [P-001](decisions/P-001-define-workshop.md): The idea establishes a free Saturday library workshop, a two-hour first session, one participant-chosen everyday digital task, mixed devices and confidence levels, and safe independent completion as the intended result.
- [P-001](decisions/P-001-define-workshop.md): Use a capped coaching workshop with advance registration and task screening; accept lower attendance in exchange for a credible chance that every participant can repeat their task independently.
- [P-001](decisions/P-001-define-workshop.md): Advertise a short supported menu and allow screened custom requests; preserve meaningful choice while redirecting requests that staff cannot prepare for, coach safely, or fit alongside an independent repeat.
