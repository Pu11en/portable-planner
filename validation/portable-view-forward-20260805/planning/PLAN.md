# Plan: One-Day Neighborhood Cleanup

## Destination

Complete a safe, one-day cleanup of the park and two nearby blocks, leaving ordinary litter sealed in bags for city collection while volunteers never handle needles or hazardous waste.

## Success

- The park and both blocks are walked once, ordinary litter is removed, hazards are left untouched and reported, all volunteers check out, and sealed bags are counted at the city-approved pickup point with collection confirmed.

## Boundaries

- In: public park paths and grounds, sidewalks and public edges on two named nearby blocks, ordinary hand-pickable litter, volunteer briefing, hazard reporting, sealed-bag staging, and city pickup handoff.
- Out: needles or sharps, chemicals, medical or biological waste, unknown liquids or containers, dead animals, bulky or heavy items, private property, traffic lanes, vegetation work, pressure washing, and hauling bags away from the city-approved point.
