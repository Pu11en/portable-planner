# P-001 — Safe Cleanup Operating Plan

- Status: complete
- Depends on: none

## Decision

Settle a complete operating route for a one-day park-and-two-block cleanup that removes ordinary litter, keeps volunteers away from hazardous material, and ends with an accountable city collection handoff.

## Viable options

- One central hub with three zone teams: easiest to supervise and reconcile, but requires enough team leads for the three zones.
- One group moving zone to zone: simplest supervision, but slower and more likely to leave volunteers idle.
- Uncoordinated self-selection: flexible, but weak coverage and safety accountability.

## Recommendation

Use one central hub and three zone teams—park, block A, and block B—with a lead for each zone, a shared stop-and-report rule, and all bags returned to one city-approved pickup point. The main tradeoff is needing three reliable leads.

## Confirmed decision

The cleanup will use a single check-in and supply hub, three small teams assigned to the park and two named blocks, and a leader for each team. Use a three-hour daylight window: 30 minutes for setup, 15 minutes for check-in and briefing, 90 minutes for zone work, 30 minutes for return and reconciliation, and 15 minutes of buffer. Volunteers collect only ordinary litter with grabbers and gloves. If anyone sees a needle, sharp, chemical, medical or biological waste, unknown liquid or container, dead animal, or other suspected hazard, nobody touches, moves, bags, guards at close range, or attempts to identify it. The team stops work in the immediate area, records the location from a safe distance, alerts the event lead, and the event lead reports it to the city-designated service or emergency service when there is immediate danger. All bags are sealed, counted, and staged only at the city-approved point. The event lead releases volunteers only after every person checks out and every zone lead reports coverage, hazards, and bag count.

## Evidence

- No external evidence needed; the user supplied the city collection arrangement and the non-handling safety boundary. Local contact details are confirmed during execution rather than guessed during planning.

## Effects

- E-001 must name the exact two blocks and confirm the city pickup point, collection window, contact, accepted bag types, and hazard-reporting channel.
- E-002 must prepare zone assignments, a roster, safety briefing, grabbers, work gloves, high-visibility gear, bags accepted by the city, first-aid supplies, water, phones, and a simple location/reporting method.
- E-003 must use buddy pairs, keep volunteers out of traffic lanes and private property, pause for severe weather or unsafe conditions, and prevent all volunteer contact with suspected hazards.
- E-004 must reconcile volunteers, zone coverage, hazard reports, bag count, and city collection confirmation.
