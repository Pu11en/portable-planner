# E-001 — Confirm Route and City Handoff

- Outcome: A written event sheet names the date, hours, hub, park boundary, two exact blocks, city pickup details, and emergency/reporting contacts.
- Depends on: P-001

## Context

- [Safe cleanup operating plan](../decisions/P-001-safe-cleanup-operating-plan.md)

## In scope

- Place the agreed three-hour operating window within safe daylight hours: 30 minutes setup, 15 minutes check-in and briefing, 90 minutes zone work, 30 minutes return and reconciliation, and 15 minutes buffer.
- Mark the public cleanup boundary for the park and each of the two blocks, excluding traffic lanes and private property.
- Confirm with the city the approved staging point, pickup window, contact, accepted bag type and weight limits, and whether bags need labels.
- Confirm the non-emergency hazard-reporting channel and the emergency escalation path.
- Record weather cancellation criteria and one communication channel for leads.

## Out of scope

- Recruiting volunteers, buying supplies, collecting litter, handling hazards, or transporting waste.

## Proof

- One event sheet contains every item above, and the city contact has acknowledged the pickup arrangement in writing or the event lead has recorded the confirmation call, time, and name.

## Human review

- Event lead approves the exact route, schedule, and city handoff before recruitment is finalized.
