# E-003 — Run the Cleanup

- Outcome: All three public zones receive one safe coverage pass and ordinary litter reaches the staging point in sealed bags.
- Depends on: E-002

## Context

- [Safe cleanup operating plan](../decisions/P-001-safe-cleanup-operating-plan.md)
- Approved event sheet, roster, zone sheets, and supply checklist from E-001 and E-002

## In scope

- Check volunteers in, issue supplies, deliver the safety briefing, and confirm that every person understands the hazard stop rule.
- Send the park, block A, and block B teams only after each lead has a roster, route, and working phone.
- Collect ordinary hand-pickable litter with grabbers and gloves while volunteers remain with buddies, on public ground, and out of traffic lanes.
- For any suspected hazard: stop nearby work, step back, prevent approach without standing close, record the location from a safe distance, notify the event lead, and resume only outside the affected area when the lead says it is safe.
- Return all teams at the scheduled cutoff; seal, label if required, count, and stage bags at the approved point.
- Pause or end the event for severe weather, traffic danger, aggressive behavior, an uncontrolled hazard, a missing volunteer, or loss of safe supervision.

## Out of scope

- Touching, moving, bagging, inspecting, or identifying suspected hazardous waste; entering private property or roads; overfilling bags; lifting heavy items; or extending the route after cutoff.

## Proof

- All volunteers check in; all three zone leads report a completed pass or document the precise section stopped for safety; hazard locations are recorded without contact; and every ordinary-litter bag is sealed, counted, and staged at the approved point.

## Human review

- Event lead verifies all people, zone reports, hazard reports, and staged bags before beginning closeout.
