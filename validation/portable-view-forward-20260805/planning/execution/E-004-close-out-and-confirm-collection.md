# E-004 — Close Out and Confirm Collection

- Outcome: Every volunteer is accounted for, hazards are reported, the site is left orderly, and city collection is confirmed.
- Depends on: E-003

## Context

- [Safe cleanup operating plan](../decisions/P-001-safe-cleanup-operating-plan.md)
- Event records and staged bags from E-003

## In scope

- Check out every volunteer and escalate immediately if anyone is unaccounted for.
- Collect zone sheets and total completed coverage, sealed bags, injuries or incidents, and untouched hazard locations.
- Submit hazard reports through the confirmed city channel; use emergency services only for immediate danger.
- Recover reusable supplies, remove loose event materials, and leave sealed bags only at the approved pickup point.
- Obtain city pickup confirmation or record the agreed pickup reference and monitoring owner if collection occurs later.
- After the collection window, verify the bags are gone; if not, contact the city and keep volunteers away from the pile.
- Send a brief thank-you and factual outcome summary after all closeout facts are known.

## Out of scope

- Volunteers guarding bags after the event, transporting bags themselves, handling reported hazards, or claiming collection before it is verified.

## Proof

- Roster shows all volunteers checked out; zone and incident records are complete; hazard reports have reference numbers or timestamps; final bag count matches staging; and city collection is confirmed or an overdue pickup escalation has a named owner and timestamp.

## Human review

- Event lead approves the closeout record and confirms no person, hazard report, or bag handoff remains unaccounted for.

## Next eligible ticket

- Plan complete.
