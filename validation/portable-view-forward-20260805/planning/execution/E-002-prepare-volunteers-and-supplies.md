# E-002 — Prepare Volunteers and Supplies

- Outcome: The event has enough briefed leaders, a zone roster, and checked supplies to run safely.
- Depends on: E-001

## Context

- [Safe cleanup operating plan](../decisions/P-001-safe-cleanup-operating-plan.md)
- Approved event sheet produced by E-001

## In scope

- Recruit one event lead, one check-in/first-aid contact, and one lead for each of the three zones; assign remaining volunteers in buddy pairs.
- Cap team size at the number the zone leads can directly account for.
- Send the date, hub, event hours, clothing guidance, weather policy, boundaries, accessibility notes, and the no-hazard-handling rule.
- Prepare a check-in/out roster and zone sheets with boundaries, leader phone numbers, hazard-location notes, and bag counts.
- Check grabbers, work gloves in multiple sizes, high-visibility gear, city-approved bags, bag ties, hand sanitizer, water, first-aid supplies, charged phones, and markers or labels if the city requires them.
- Give zone leads the same short briefing: ordinary litter only; stay with a buddy; stay off private property and traffic lanes; stop, step back, mark the location remotely, and report suspected hazards; return by the stated time.

## Out of scope

- Specialized sharps equipment, hazard cleanup, unsupervised minors, litter collection, or city waste transport.

## Proof

- Every required role is named, every volunteer is assigned to a zone and buddy or support role, all supplies pass a checklist, and each zone lead confirms the briefing and escalation method.

## Human review

- Event lead signs the roster and supply checklist before event-day check-in opens.
