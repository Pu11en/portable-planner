# P-001 — Define the first user and evidence job

- Status: current
- Depends on: none

## Decision

Choose the first intended user and the real-world job they need transcript evidence to support. This sets the meaning of useful search, the information each result must show, and the proof that the app works.

## Viable options

- Product researchers reviewing customer interviews: optimize for finding recurring needs and reopening exact supporting passages; focused and straightforward, but narrower than general qualitative research.
- Founders or consultants reviewing discovery calls: optimize for quickly grounding product or client decisions; accessible, but their workflows vary widely.
- Journalists or academic researchers: optimize for rigorous citation, coding, and traceability; evidence quality is clear, but the first version may need more specialized features.

## Recommendation

Start with product researchers reviewing customer interviews. It gives the app one crisp evidence job and a practical usefulness test, at the cost of postponing broader research workflows.

## Confirmed decision

Not confirmed.

## Evidence

- No external evidence needed; this is a product-direction preference.

## Effects

- Determines the first workflow, result format, sample transcripts, and acceptance test.
- May reveal whether later planning must split for factual research or a disposable search comparison.

## Complete when

The first intended user and the decision or task their transcript evidence must support are explicit, and `PLAN.md` reflects that direction without contradiction.
