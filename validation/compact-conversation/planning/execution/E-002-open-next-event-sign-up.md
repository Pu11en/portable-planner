# E-002 — Open the Next Event Sign-Up

- Outcome: The next skill-share has a published invitation and an open list for learners and volunteer teachers.
- Depends on: P-001, E-001

## Context

- [Confirmed participation experience](../decisions/P-001-define-participation-experience.md)
- [Reusable event kit](E-001-prepare-reusable-event-kit.md)

## In scope

- Confirm the free library room date and applicable room rules directly with the library.
- Publish the approved invitation through the neighborhood's existing free channels.
- Open the role sign-up with a clear cutoff before the event.
- Track teacher offers, learner sign-ups, first-time status when volunteered, activity materials, and accommodation notes in the reusable roster.

## Out of scope

- Selecting the three stations, assigning groups, purchasing materials, or running the event.

## Constraints

- The invitation must say that this is guided practical learning, not a networking event.
- Attendance is free; teachers and participants bring any materials their activities require.
- No new paid tool, advertising channel, or registration system may be introduced.

## Proof

- The invitation is live with the correct date, place, duration, no-cost promise, role choices, materials expectation, and cutoff; responses land in one usable roster.

## If blocked or disproven

- If the room is unavailable or its rules prevent the planned activities, stop before publishing and return the exact constraint to planning. If responses are simply low, use a targeted reminder through the same free channels.

## Human review

- The host verifies the published details and sign-up destination.
