# E-003 — Lock Stations and Group Routes

- Outcome: Three compatible teaching stations and three balanced learner routes are confirmed for the next event.
- Depends on: P-001, E-002

## Context

- [Confirmed participation experience](../decisions/P-001-define-participation-experience.md)
- [Reusable event kit](E-001-prepare-reusable-event-kit.md)
- The completed roster from E-002

## In scope

- Select three volunteer teachers whose activities each produce one small result in 20 minutes and fit the library's confirmed rules.
- Confirm every teacher's material instructions, participant capacity, setup, and pass/observe option.
- Divide signed-up learners into three balanced small groups and give each group a route through all three stations.
- Prepare the room layout, arrival assignments, teacher reminders, attendee materials reminder, and walk-in placement rule.

## Out of scope

- Changing the guided-round format, supplying paid materials, delivering the lessons, or running the event.

## Constraints

- First-time attendees receive an assigned starting group and are never required to teach or address the full room.
- Each learner group visits all three stations once.
- Activities remain practical, non-promotional, and possible with participant-supplied materials.

## Proof

- The final run sheet names three confirmed teachers and outcomes, lists material instructions, assigns every signed-up learner to a balanced route, covers walk-ins, and fits the confirmed 90-minute agenda.

## If blocked or disproven

- If fewer than three suitable teachers confirm, repeat the targeted teacher request or move the event; do not replace a missing station with unstructured networking. Return to planning only if three-station guided rounds repeatedly cannot be staffed.

## Human review

- The host approves the final station lineup, room fit, material instructions, and group routes.
