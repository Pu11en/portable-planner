# E-004 — Run the Guided Skill-Share

- Outcome: One 90-minute guided skill-share is completed and attendee responses are collected.
- Depends on: P-001, E-003

## Context

- [Confirmed participation experience](../decisions/P-001-define-participation-experience.md)
- [Final stations and group routes](E-003-lock-stations-and-groups.md)

## In scope

- Set up the three stations and the arrival assignment before attendees enter.
- Run the paired welcome from minutes 0–10 and explain the three routes from minutes 10–15.
- Keep the three station rounds to minutes 15–35, 35–55, and 55–75.
- Close and collect the response check from minutes 75–85.
- Reset the library room from minutes 85–90.
- Record attendance, first-time responses, comfort responses, return intent, timing failures, and material or room problems.

## Out of scope

- Adding open networking time, changing the event format during the session, purchasing materials, or analyzing the results.

## Constraints

- Attendees may observe or pass; no one is forced to teach, speak to the full room, or join an activity.
- Transitions remain host-led and every attendee has an assigned next station.
- The room is restored by minute 90.

## Proof

- The host run sheet records actual timing and attendance; all three rounds occurred; the room was reset; and the collected responses identify first-time status, comfort, and return intent.

## If blocked or disproven

- Preserve attendee comfort and library rules first. Record deviations instead of improvising networking. Any safety, access, timing, or format failure is carried into E-005.

## Human review

- The host signs off on the completed run sheet and records any deviation while it is fresh.
