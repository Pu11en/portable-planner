# E-005 — Review Results and Prepare the Next Month

- Outcome: The event's success is measured and one evidence-based keep-or-adjust decision is recorded for the next month.
- Depends on: P-001, E-004

## Context

- [Confirmed participation experience](../decisions/P-001-define-participation-experience.md)
- The attendance, response check, and deviation notes from E-004

## In scope

- Calculate the share of responding first-time attendees who felt comfortable joining in.
- Calculate the share of all respondents who said they would return.
- Compare the results with the 70% comfort and 50% return thresholds.
- Record what to keep and at most one small operational adjustment for the next month.
- Carry that adjustment into the reusable kit and identify E-002 as the next cycle's starting point.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the guided-round format without returning to planning, adding paid resources, or making several untestable changes at once.

## Constraints

- A missing first-time response means the comfort goal was not proven, not that it passed.
- Preserve the confirmed no-budget, participant-materials, newcomer-comfort, and no-networking boundaries.

## Proof

- A short review records both numerators and denominators, pass/fail against both thresholds, observed problems, the one keep-or-adjust decision, and the next month's action.

## If blocked or disproven

- If the guided format misses either threshold in two consecutive events, or the needed adjustment would change the confirmed participation experience, return to planning with both event reviews.

## Human review

- The host approves the recorded adjustment before it changes the reusable kit.

## Next eligible ticket

- E-002 — Open the Next Event Sign-Up for the next monthly cycle, or return to planning under the stated failure condition.
