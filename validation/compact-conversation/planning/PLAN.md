# Plan: Neighborhood Skill-Share Night

**Status:** awaiting approval

## Destination

A repeatable plan for a free monthly, 90-minute library skill-share where about 20 adults teach one another practical skills and shy first-time attendees can participate comfortably.

## Success

- After each event, at least 70% of first-time attendees say they felt comfortable joining in, and at least half say they would return.

## Boundaries

- In: Participation format, 90-minute flow, newcomer comfort, participant-led materials, host responsibilities, and a simple success check.
- Out: Paid space or supplies, organizer-provided activity materials, generic networking activities, and changes to the fixed room, duration, group size, or no-budget boundary.
