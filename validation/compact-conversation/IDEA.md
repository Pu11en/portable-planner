# Neighborhood Skill-Share Night

Plan a monthly, 90-minute neighborhood skill-share night for about 20 adults in a free library room.

## Confirmed context

- People teach one another small practical skills.
- Shy first-time attendees should feel comfortable participating.
- It must not feel like a generic networking event.
- The library room, event length, group size, and no-budget boundary are fixed.
- Participants bring any materials their activity needs.
- Success means at least 70% of first-time attendees say they felt comfortable joining in and at least half say they would return.

## Human-owned uncertainty

Drew has not decided how structured participation should feel. Several viable formats could satisfy the goal, so the planner should make the abstract difference concrete before asking for that preference.
